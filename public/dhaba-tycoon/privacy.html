1. Summary

Dhaba Tycoon is a free game made by Garv AI Labs. It uses a random player ID, basic game and device information, Google AdMob for ads, Google Play for purchases, and Google Analytics for Firebase and Meta to measure our own ads for the game. This page explains exactly what is used and why.

4. Ads

The game shows rewarded ads that you choose to watch, for example for free coins or a 2× income boost. Google AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and IP address (which gives an approximate location, never your GPS position) to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. The game does not use your phone's location permission.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your phone's settings (Settings → Google → Ads). The ad-free plan (a one-time purchase) removes ads.

5. Purchases

Coin packs and the ad-free plan are sold through Google Play. The ad-free plan is a one-time purchase: pay once and it stays on your Google account, including on a new phone. Payment, receipts and refunds are handled by Google Play under its own terms. Coins and gems are play money and cannot be exchanged for real money.

6. Notifications

If you allow them, the game sends a few reminders, for example when a daily reward is ready. You can turn them off at any time in your phone's settings.

7. Keeping & deleting data

Game and device information is kept while you play and for up to 24 months after you last play, then deleted.

To have your data deleted sooner, email us the player ID shown in the game's Settings. We delete it within 30 days. Uninstalling the game removes the copy on your phone; a Google Play Games save can be removed from your Google account settings.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top always shows the latest version, and important changes will also be shown in the game.

10. Contact

Questions about your data, or a deletion request? We usually reply within a few working days.
